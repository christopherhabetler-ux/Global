## 1. Why

The goal is to grow EdTech Expertz, in revenue and in impact. Right now the constraint is your time. You're uniquely qualified to do this work, the business is ready to grow, and nobody has a time machine. The right level and type of support, from someone you know, lets you take on AAP and more clients. This is a first step toward that.

What I'm aiming for:

- A clear working arrangement. We've both said we want to work together, and I want the terms to match.
- Helping you build EdTech Expertz into the empire you've talked about.
- The just-right level of support for this moment, adjusted as the business grows.
- A setup where you feel completely comfortable asking me for whatever you need, and I earn my place on your team.

## 4. The Numbers

### How I Got to $3,000

These are rough figures from our conversations that I think I have right. Please correct me where I don't.

| Your business | Your monthly profit, as I understood it |
| --- | --- |
| Three clients (today) | About $3,000 past your bills |
| Four clients (with AAP) | About $12,000 |
| A few more, within six months | About $30,000 |

Read that way, each new client is worth roughly $6,000 to $9,000 a month in profit to you. $3,000 is between a third and a half of one. The job is helping you take on and carry the next one.

### How the Pay Works

| Piece | Amount | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base rate | $3,000 a month | Starts October 1 or the day AAP signs, whichever is later. The work starts when the base rate does. |
| New clients | +15% of each new client's monthly retainer | A $10,000-a-month client adds $1,500, bringing the base rate to $4,500. Tell me the amount when a retainer signs, and I'll invoice the new number. |
| Referral network | 20% of what EdTech Expertz keeps on network deals | Your 9/16 idea: pay that "grows as the sales these individuals bring in grows." I'll never pitch members, and my own intros stay under our August agreement. |
| Projects, courses, speaking | Agreed before each one starts | Only if I help build it |
| Already in motion | No charge | Benchmark, and Forward Edge on 10/1 |

Why these percentages:

- 15% is the top of the 10% to 15% range referral partners typically earn on new business. It leaves 85% of every new client with EdTech Expertz.
- 20% is higher because it applies only to network deals, which I'd be running day to day. I don't know what you usually keep on a network deal, so this is the number most likely to change.

If this is too big a swing to start, let's find a smaller one. From what I understand, you could use the support, and I sure wouldn't mind a little income.

| January | Our first real review: what I delivered, what it freed up for you, and whether the rate and the time still match the work |
| After that | Quarterly, or anytime either of us wants |

| If | Then |
