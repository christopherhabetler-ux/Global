## How It Grows

This is your "floor bumps up, percentage bumps up" idea, with the triggers written down. The same rules apply to all three options.

- The base grows with new clients. Each new retainer client you sign adds 15% of that client's monthly fee to my base, until it reaches $5,000. A client the size of ClassE adds about $1,275, so two like that would get us to the full rate. Smaller clients move it less. If a client that moved the base leaves, the base steps back down with it.
- The percentage grows with the network. Once network deals bring EdTech Expertz more than $25,000 in a year, 20% becomes 25%.
- Bigger projects get their own number. If a client pays you for work I do most of, we agree on my share before it starts.
- January is the "bang-up job" check. We sit down and look at what I delivered (leads opened, the health of the network, the research you actually used) along with the numbers, and reset anything that needs it.

A network deal means a deal you close with a company through a member intro that you approved, logged in the tracker when you approved it. I'll never pitch members, since you've been clear this can't turn into a sales engine. My own intros stay under the August agreement, and if an intro fits both, the August agreement applies, so nothing gets paid twice.

Either of us can end this with 30 days' notice, and you'd get a clean handoff. The network share keeps paying for 12 months on deals already logged.
