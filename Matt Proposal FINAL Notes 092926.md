- The market evidence is rated weak to moderate on that page. That's why the proposal states $5,000 as your number and not as "the market rate."
- The $2,500 start still checks out against Matt's own numbers: 13-15% of the $18K a month gain he described is $2,340-$2,700.
- Growth runs at 15% of each new client's monthly fee. That's the same share as Herk's band, it scales with client size, and it never takes a disproportionate bite of a small client.
