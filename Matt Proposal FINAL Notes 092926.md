## How The Price Was Set

- $5,000 a month is the full rate, taken from PAY ANCHORS 091626. A network operator retainer runs $3-8K a month for 1-2 days a week (ChatGPT) or $2K / $3.5-5.5K / $8K (Gemini). Your own target is $150-200 an hour. The Vendux 2025 fractional go-to-market average is $11,732 a month. $5,000 sits in the middle of the operator band and well under the fractional average.
- The market evidence is rated weak to moderate on that page. That's why the proposal states $5,000 as your number and not as "the market rate."
- The $2,500 start still checks out against Matt's own numbers: 13-15% of the $18K a month gain he described is $2,340-$2,700.
- Growth runs at 15% of each new client's monthly fee. That's the same share as Herk's band, it scales with client size, and it never takes a disproportionate bite of a small client.
- One planning assumption stays off the page: the full rate assumes about a day a week. The weekly check-in is where you'd notice if the load runs well past that.

## Your Partnership Framing, My Reaction

It's right, and it's better than the package. Matt asked for someone with "true ownership" who comes back with more than asked, and caps on reports undercut exactly that.

The risk is that an open scope at $2,500 becomes unlimited work at a fixed price. Caps aren't the fix. Two lines in the final handle it instead: the weekly check-in where he sets priorities, and "if the work ever outgrows the pay, or the pay outgrows the work, we'll both see it there." The second half of that sentence is what keeps it from reading as a hedge.
