Matt and Joe,

Attached is the current working draft of the US language guide. It's meant to guide us in making language choices that accurately reflect ClassE and what we believe about students and teachers. It is not a script, and we will keep updating it.

It proposes moving away from a few words we have been using:
- We lead with what ClassE does instead of calling it diagnostic or an intervention. Intervention becomes a two-part answer: ClassE helps the teacher intervene, and Albert gives each student targeted support directly.
- We say "progress toward mastery" instead of "mastery" off a single question, including on the report.
- Before a call, we check the district's student-facing AI policies to decide how much Albert becomes a selling point.

Most of the "sounds like" lines come from our own calls, including mine.

Take a look and let me know where you'd go a different way.
