# Zeta Live Coach 092926

Same design as LIVE CALL COACH 081426: an interrupt coach on your Mac that watches a live transcript file and flashes one short alert when you say a trigger phrase, plus clock alerts at minutes 1, 8, 11, 20, 26 and 29. No model in the loop, so there's no delay.

## Tested
Replayed against your side of the Dan Rojas call (139 lines, 092526): 19 alerts. They include every defect in the Rojas debrief: the health reason, "better part of a decade," "we were able to be successful," grading KIPP and AF, "I wish I had the answer," "the rest of the mess," "blah, blah, blah," "earn that" with no investment line, and no role question by minute 10.

## What it needs on the Mac (the open item from 081426)
1. Both sides of the Zoom audio routed to one input: BlackHole plus an Aggregate Device (steps in LIVE CALL COACH setup 081426).
2. A transcriber that appends to a text file while you talk (whisper.cpp streaming is the reliable one).
   To test it: run `wc -c ~/transcripts/live.txt` twice, 30 seconds apart, while you talk. If the two numbers are different, it works.

## Run
```
python3 "zeta coach.py" --demo
python3 "zeta coach.py" --transcript ~/transcripts/live.txt
python3 "zeta coach.py" --transcript "some old transcript.txt" --replay
```
