### Architecture 1: in-session Claude copilot reading the live Notion transcript (the one that worked)
**Setup, from the FC LIVE COPILOT prompt 081426 (3bc05467120c8102b5f0d4154038cd65):**
1. "Paste the block below into a NEW Opus session (Cowork, Career project, folders connected) at about 11:35 AM" (10 minutes before the call).

3. Transcript arrives by paste, or "a file that my transcription app is appending to … poll it every 20 to 30 seconds with a shell read of the tail."
4. Output shapes only: `→ SAY: "<exact words, under 15>"` · `⚠ <three to six words>` · `# <the number>` · `✓ landed` · `STOP`. "Three lines, maximum. Usually one." "Silence is a valid output."
5. Watch-list (landmines) and push-list (openings), plus a close prompt at about minute 25.
6. On `DONE`: questions verbatim, what landed and what didn't, commitments, numbers, a four-sentence thank-you draft, TODAY/PIPELINE lines, then write the debrief to disk and Notion.

**How it actually ran (081726 DREAM, 081826 FC Part 2, 082426 S2):**

**What worked:**

PROOF.` · visible counter plus clock · must-say list that visibly empties · the last 10 minutes are verbal rehearsal (two sentences, three times each), not document review · split the prep companion from a one-screen live surface.
