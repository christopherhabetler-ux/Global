Matt, thank you for asking. I've loved working with you these last few months, and I appreciate the trust behind it. On the 22nd you asked what I could do, what it would look like, and what I would charge. I'll admit I'm a little nervous putting numbers on paper, so forgive anything that seems presumptuous. It only works for me if it works for you.

The Opportunity

EdTech Expertz is ready to grow: AAP is about to sign, five strong leads are waiting, and you've talked about building it into an empire. The constraint is your time. You're uniquely qualified, and nobody has a time machine. If I take enough off your plate, the business brings in more while your hours stay about the same, and I earn a real place on the team.

What I'd Take Off Your Plate

ResearchCrosswalks, diligence readouts, prep briefs on prospective clients, and people maps (your "30 people at Canvas"). You set the goal, and I come back with more than you asked for.
AdvisoryThe school-side voice in your client work: first meetings with prospective clients, working sessions, messaging, and feedback on decks and pitches.
Referral networkRecruiting members, training them on the companies, and checking in on them. Members and clients still go through you.
DelegationThe consulting infrastructure you mentioned: the ChatGPT workspace, a shared knowledge base, the company email, and QuickBooks.

What It Frees You to Do

  • The strategy and product work only you can do
  • Take on AAP and open the five parked leads
  • Room to think, and to hand things off with confidence

The Value I'd Bring

  • School-side credibility in the room with your clients
  • A referral network that runs day to day without you
  • Client context in one shared place, not only in your head

How I Got to a Number

  1. Price the value, not the hours. No hourly rate, and no cap on what you can ask for.
  2. Start where the business is today. $3,000 a month is between a third and a half of what one new client is worth to you.
  3. Grow only when the business grows. 15% of each new client's monthly retainer, the top of the 10% to 15% range referral partners typically earn. The other 85% stays with EdTech Expertz.
  4. Tie network pay to network results. 20% of what EdTech Expertz keeps on network deals, your 9/16 idea of pay that "grows as the sales these individuals bring in grows."

$3,000 a month to start, growing as EdTech Expertz grows.

If that's too big a swing to start, let's find a smaller one. From what I understand, you could use the support, and I sure wouldn't mind a little income.
