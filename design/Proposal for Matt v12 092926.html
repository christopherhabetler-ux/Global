Matt, thank you for asking. I've loved working with you these last few months, and I appreciate the trust behind it.

I'll admit I'm a little nervous putting numbers on paper, so forgive anything that seems presumptuous. On the 22nd you asked what I could do, what it would look like, and what I would charge. This is my first stab at an answer. I'm excited about it, I expect we'll change a good bit of it together, and it only works for me if it works for you.

The Opportunity

EdTech Expertz is ready to grow. AAP is about to sign, five strong leads are waiting, and you've talked about building it into an empire. Right now, you're the bottleneck. You're amazing at this work, so everything runs through you, and nobody has a time machine. If I take enough off your plate, the business can take on more clients while your hours stay about the same.

How I Got to a Number

I had Claude work from what you walked me through last week, then went back and forth with what it came up with until it felt fair, appropriate, and clear. The pay has three parts.

  1. A base rate. I priced the value, not the hours, so there's no hourly rate and no cap on what you can ask for. One new client is worth about $6,000 to $9,000 a month to you, and a third to a half of that is $3,000 a month.
  2. A share of new clients. The pay should grow only when the business grows. Referral partners typically earn 10% to 15% of new business, and I took the top of that range: 15% of each new client's monthly retainer. The other 85% stays with EdTech Expertz.
  3. A share of the referral network. On 9/16 you described pay that "grows as the sales these individuals bring in grows." Running the network would be my job, so when a deal comes in through a member, I'd get 20% of what EdTech Expertz keeps on that deal.

Putting it all together

$3,000 a month to start, growing only as EdTech Expertz grows.

Items for Conversation

  1. A full-time role. I'm in late rounds for a full-time role at a charter network, and there may be others. If one happens, you'll hear it from me first, and I want to keep doing both, with the work before and after the school day.
