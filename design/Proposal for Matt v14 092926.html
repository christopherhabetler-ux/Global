04How I Got to a Number

I had Claude start from what you walked me through last week, then went back and forth with it until the numbers felt fair and clear. The pay has three parts.

  1. A base rate. Priced on value, not hours. $3,000 a month is a third to half of what one new client adds. At three clients it would be your whole margin, so it starts when AAP signs.
  2. A share of new clients. New clients come from your relationships and your selling. My part is helping you win and carry them, which is a referral partner's role, and referral partners typically earn 10% to 15%. I took the top of that range: 15% of each new client's monthly retainer.
  3. A share of the referral network. On 9/16 you described pay that "grows as the sales these individuals bring in grows." Here I'd do most of the work behind the deal, recruiting and training the members who bring it. So the share is higher, 20%, but of what EdTech Expertz keeps on the deal, not the full deal.

Putting it all together: $3,000 a month to start, growing only as EdTech Expertz grows.
